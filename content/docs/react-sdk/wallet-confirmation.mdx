---
title: Confirm a returning wallet
description: Verify a returning Protocol wallet before adopting it.
---

A returning passkey can have more than one wallet candidate. Keep the established
wallet PDA and its cluster in application state. Validate that the wallet account
belongs to the configured program and the authority belongs to the expected wallet.
The selected adapter version determines how its wallet confirmation UI and callbacks work.

Verify the relying-party domain during the passkey ceremony. Do not use a credential
lookup alone to decide that a wallet belongs to a returning user.

See [Smart wallet](/concepts/smart-wallet) and [Networks](/networks).
