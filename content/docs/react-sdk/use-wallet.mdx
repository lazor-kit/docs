---
title: Use a Protocol wallet
description: Connect, approve and delegate with a compatible wallet adapter.
---

Call `useWallet` from your provider and confirm the installed `@lazorkit/wallet` version
supports your selected [Protocol deployment](/networks).

## Connect

Reconnect to the wallet the application established for this passkey and cluster.
Inspect the vault PDA before showing a receive address. Check the selected wallet
before sending, creating a session or updating an authority.

## Send a transaction

Build the intended Solana instructions and call your adapter's passkey send method.
Make the amount and recipient clear before opening the portal. Retain the returned
signature to confirm the result or check an uncertain relay response.

## Sessions and authorities

Use an Owner or Admin to register or revoke a [session](/concepts/session-keys).
Use [authority roles](/concepts/rbac) for wallet management. Attach spending
actions to a session key and check its expiry slot. Match a stored session signing
key to its wallet before signing.

For exact signatures and overloads, see your installed adapter's type definitions
or [build instructions directly](/web3js-v1).
