title: Protocol overview
description: How LazorKit wallets sign transactions, delegate spending, and pay fees.
---

A LazorKit wallet has two addresses. The **wallet PDA** identifies the wallet and its authorities. The **vault PDA** holds SOL and tokens. Show the vault address when the user wants to receive funds.

## Approve and execute

| Path | Who signs | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Direct execution](/web3js-v1/passkey-signing) | A wallet authority with a passkey or an Ed25519 key | The signer approves the action now using `Execute`. |
| [Session key](/concepts/session-keys) | A registered Ed25519 session key | An app runs delegated actions until the session expires or is revoked. |
| [Deferred execution](/web3js-v1/passkey-signing#deferred-execution) | A wallet authority approves with a passkey | Approval and submission must happen at different times. The approval expires at its configured slot. |

[Authority roles](/concepts/rbac) determine who can manage the wallet. Spending limits and program filters are configured on session keys.

## Who pays

The transaction's fee payer pays Solana's network fee. A [paymaster](/concepts/paymaster) can take that role for the user. Wallet creation and execution can also incur a Protocol fee in SOL. Account creation can require rent.
